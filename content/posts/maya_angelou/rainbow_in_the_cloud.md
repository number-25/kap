A few notes from Maya Angelou's *Rainbow in the Cloud* 

---

The Black Child must learn early to allow laughter  
to fill his mouth or the million small cruelties he  
encounters will congeal and clog his throat.

**Awien commented on how "some people don't survive being black, some people cannot take it"**

What child can resist a mother who laughs freely and often?

**Reminds me of the expression; "the greatest gift that you can give your child is your happiness"**

There is no greater agony than bearing an untold story inside of you.

**One can seemingly flick to any page in this collection and find a gem to sit on.**
